Notes

  • Source: DAIR Student Enrollment dashboard, Data Table tab (Term = Fall 2026), rows with College = School of Information Sciences. Retrieved October 14, 2026. Counts are headcounts on the 10th day of instruction (census), as the dashboard's About tab defines them.

  • PhD Information Sciences includes 1 student still listed under the program's former name, PhD in Library & Information Science. The program was renamed in a Board of Trustees item dated November 14, 2019, and DAIR first lists the new program code in Fall 2020.
  • DAIR's Data Table lists the PhD in Informatics and the MS in Game Development twice, once under each of two department names (Informatics and Illinois Informatics Institute). Each student is counted once here, so the programs add up to DAIR's college total: 981 graduate + 859 undergraduate = 1,840.
  • MS Bioinformatics counts only the Information Sciences concentration, the one housed in the iSchool. Graduate non-degree students are in the online Library & Information Science option.
